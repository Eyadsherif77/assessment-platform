Official Cost Architecture & Scalability Matrix

Platform: Educational Assessment & Diagnostic Platform • Scope: 0 to 1,000,000 Active Students
Production Architecture • TiDB Cloud Serverless + Google Gemini AI
Key Clarification (Why $1,030 vs. <$5.00?):
• The $1,030/mo figure represented a worst-case theoretical scenario where 5% of 10,000,000 monthly quizzes (500,000 live API calls) bypass the cache and hit Gemini dynamically every month.
• The <$5.00/mo figure represents our real-world Question Bank architecture: The Egyptian curriculum is finite (~300 textbook chapters). Once AI generates ~50-100 questions per chapter into TiDB, 1,000,000 students take quizzes directly from the database without invoking live Gemini API calls.
